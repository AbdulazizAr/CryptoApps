CryptoApps

Thirteen interactive tools for exploring classical ciphers, block ciphers, and hash functions. Enter your own values and follow the calculations.

Tools & examplesSource & documentation

Block ciphers

AES-128 block explorer ↗

Encrypt and decrypt a single block with a complete round trace.

AES key expansion ↗

Explore all ten derived AES-128 round keys.

AES operations ↗

Experiment with SubBytes, ShiftRows, MixColumns, and their inverses.

DES calculator ↗

Inspect encryption, decryption, key scheduling, and round values.

DES operations ↗

Visualize permutations, the Feistel function, S-boxes, and subkeys.

Simplified DES ↗

Study the two-round teaching cipher with an 8-bit block and 10-bit key.

Classical ciphers

Caesar cipher ↗

Shift a custom alphabet, decrypt, or recover a known shift.

Substitution cipher ↗

Encrypt and decrypt using a permutation of your alphabet.

Vigenère cipher ↗

Use a repeating key or recover a plaintext/ciphertext keystream.

One-time pad demonstration ↗

Explore modular addition with a key as long as the message.

Hill cipher ↗

Explore 3×3 matrices, determinants, and modular inverses.

Hash functions

MD5 visualizer ↗

Inspect message padding, compression steps, and the final digest.

SHA-1 visualizer ↗

Explore message schedules, compression rounds, and digests.

Built for cryptography coursework and experimentation. These tools are educational demonstrations, not a system for protecting real secrets. Calculations run in your browser; some tools save inputs locally.
